What to try

The standardisation layer ships first. This prototype shows the admin screens and a sample consumer (a dashboard, a report and Ask AI) reading the standardised data index. The calendar is the next phase and has its own prototype.

  • work by brand and status, the report and Ask AI, all read from the index.
  • Legal #88 isn’t counted because Legal review’s “Legal review” status isn’t mapped. Follow “Map it”, map it to Review, then come back: the count goes up.
  • confirms four suggestions in one go: Title on Legal review, Channel on Creative request, End on Event brief and Budget on Campaign Creation. Then ask about budget.
  • Event brief’s Start field was removed. Map Event start date and the gap disappears from the dashboard.
  • descriptions for people and AI, renaming without breaking reports, the Number type and list-value colours.
  • no toggle and nothing mandatory; status mapping and field tags.
  • a viewer who can’t open Legal review workflows gets different counts, charts and AI answers.
